The largest removal in the token's newest 100 rows, followed on this chain, through a keyless serverless proxy (CoinMarketCap sends no CORS header, so the browser cannot call it directly). The proxy shares one IP across every visitor, and the anonymous tier throttles per IP — if it says so, that is the tier, not the tool: run the CLI, where every reader has their own quota, for 300 rows and the cross-chain version.

What it refuses

  • Just-in-time pairs. A transaction that both adds and removes is not an event. It is discarded before the trigger, and naming one by hash returns a refusal, not a verdict (DAI $229,834, jit.json ↗).
  • Slivers. A removal below 10% of its pool is large in dollars and small for the pool — skipped, with the share printed.
  • Solana as a cross-chain destination. A Solana maker is not an EVM address; it cannot be this wallet.
  • Chains with no market. An asset with under $10k of DEX liquidity on a chain is not followed there — stated, not silently skipped.
  • A throttled follow is never an Exit. If any planned follow fails, the verdict is INCOMPLETE and says so (invariant I6).

Honest limits

  • Pool identity is venue + pair, not pool address. Rows carry no pool address; two fee tiers of one pair on one venue collapse into one identity.
  • The window is ±6 h. A wallet that comes back a day later reads as an Exit for that window — the card says which window it searched.
  • A wallet is not an entity. Liquidity moved through a second wallet is not followed; the share is a floor.
  • A wallet that cycles more than once in the window can show a share over 100% — the adds are attributed to the whole window, and the card discloses the other removals.
  • This page is a dated receipt. Your numbers will differ — re-run it.
