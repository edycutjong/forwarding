The API

6 keyless endpoints. One parameter is the whole join.

Per liquidity event, keyless, CoinMarketCap publishes the maker's own wallet — and /v1/dex/liquidity-change/list accepts maker= as a server-side filter: one call returns that wallet's adds and removes across every pool of the token. The other 5 endpoints supply the pools, the cross-chain identity, the depth and the card's header.

finding 2 · just-in-time
118 transactions

118 of the sweep's 1,852 rows were a transaction that both adds and removes (97 on DAI, 21 on CAKE). Counted as removals they make every alert a Rebalance. A JIT pair is discarded before the trigger, and naming one by hash returns a refusal, not a verdict.

5 keyless investigations, one IP. The transcript below took 50.6 s: the anonymous tier reports its per-IP limit as error 1022, 1011 or 500 with no Retry-After, and the run hit 1 backoff of 15 s. The tool backs off 15 / 30 / 60 s, records every retry, and exits 75 when the quota is spent.

Questions a judge will ask

Honest limits.

Is this page live?

The verdict is rendered from a committed run, captured 2026-09-18 23:16:31 UTC, and says so on every number. The paste box is live: the same engine, same-chain, through a keyless proxy that shares one IP across every visitor, so a 429 is shown as a 429 with the command to run instead. The CLI is the primary live path — every reader has their own quota there.

How wide is the window?

±6 h around the removal, walked with the cursor because startTime is plan-gated on the keyless surface. A wallet that comes back a day later reads as an Exit for that window, and the card says which window it searched. Adds before the removal count too, so a wallet that cycles more than once inside the window can show a share above 100 % — the verdict counts and discloses the other removals.

Is a verdict a judgment on a person?

No. A wallet is not an entity: liquidity moved through a second wallet is not followed, and the share is a floor. The page prints the two rows and the rule; the number is "the same maker address on both rows", exactly as the API reports it, and nothing more is claimed.

Why does the card say the share of pool is unknown?

Rows carry no pool address, so a pool is (venue, pair) and two fee tiers of one pair collapse into one identity. The removal's pool was not among the 20 that token/pools returns, so its depth now is unknown — and the page refuses to print a number no row supports: make check fails on "0.0 % of the pool". Both are findings in FEEDBACK.md.

What does the agent refuse?

A transaction that both adds and removes (118 of the sweep's 1,852 rows); a removal under 10 % of its pool, with the share printed; rows above $10B (13 in the sweep, price-feed artefacts); Solana as a cross-chain destination, because a Solana maker cannot be this wallet; a chain where the asset holds under $10k of liquidity. A throttled follow is INCOMPLETE, never an Exit.

What is not built?

No prediction, no wallet labelling, no second-wallet following, no swap analysis, no composite score, no key. The product follows the wallet and rewrites one alert; that verb is the whole scope. When nothing comes back the verdict says Exit with the window it searched — the rule is printed, never widened.
